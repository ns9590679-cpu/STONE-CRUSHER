01 — The Company

Built on
bedrock.

VAJRA began in 2004 with a single jaw crusher and a promise: material India's builders could trust without question. Two decades on, we operate four integrated crushing units across Madhya Pradesh, feeding highways, metro corridors, and townships with graded aggregate.

Every stone we ship is quarried, crushed, screened, and tested under one roof — no middlemen, no compromise. It's why contractors who've built India's biggest infrastructure projects call us first.

02 — Materials

Graded for
every pour.

MACHINE / 01

Primary Jaw Crusher

1000×650mm feed opening. Reduces quarry boulders up to 800mm to manageable primary feed at 150 TPH.

MACHINE / 02

Cone Crusher (Secondary)

Hydraulic cone unit refines primary output into 20–40mm graded stone with tight tolerance control.

MACHINE / 03

Triple-Deck Vibrating Screen

Segregates output into precise size fractions simultaneously — 40mm, 20mm, 10mm and dust.

MACHINE / 04

Wheel Loaders & Tippers

Fleet of loaders and 60+ tippers move graded material from stockpile straight to your site.

03 — The Process

From boulder
to building block.

01
⛰

Raw Stone

Basalt boulders are blasted and excavated from our owned quarry faces, then hauled to the primary crusher yard.

02
⚙

Crushing

Jaw and cone crushers reduce boulders in two stages, breaking material down to workable aggregate sizes.

03
▦

Screening

Triple-deck vibrating screens separate crushed stone into precise, certified size fractions.

04
◈

Sorting

Graded material is stockpiled by size and type, quality-checked, and staged for dispatch.

05
🚛

Transportation

Loaded directly onto our fleet and tracked in real time until it reaches your site.
